THE ACTION DIFFERENCE

Beyond chatbots that only generate text.

Traditional bots answer FAQs and leave data entry to your team. Qefro connects directly to your software to complete operational tasks.

Standard FAQ Chatbots

Answers questions only

Requires your team to manually copy data, open tabs, and finish the job.

  • Replies with static text: Sends fixed answers and directs users to phone lines or static links.
  • Requires manual follow-up: Staff must still open tabs, check software records, and key in data manually.
  • Disconnected from back-office data: Does not check live stock, update customer ledgers, or write to your systems.
  • Unstructured output: May provide unverified replies if source knowledge is outdated.
Qefro AI Business Platform

Completes the business action

Understands customer intent, validates system rules, and runs the workflow.

  • Executes the transaction: Checks system availability, books slots, and issues immediate confirmations.
  • Two-way software integration: Reads and updates records in your ERP, CRM, or Google Sheets with detailed audit logs.
  • Schema-validated workflows: Actions follow strict business rules with optional human approval for sensitive tasks.
  • Payment and invoicing follow-through: Dispatches invoice details with direct payment links and updates records upon settlement.

ANSWERS

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know about safety, pricing, integration, and ownership.

What if the AI makes a mistake or gives the wrong price?

Qefro does not "improvise" pricing or business terms. The AI runs on deterministic rule engines. It queries your verified database or spreadsheet for exact SKU rates and policies. Furthermore, you can configure hard guardrails so that any action involving financial changes or discounts requires one-click employee authorization.

Can my team review and approve actions before they are sent?

Yes. You can enable "Staff Approval Mode" on any workflow. For example, Qefro can draft a formal GST invoice or payment settlement and notify your accounts manager on WhatsApp or portal with an [Approve / Edit] button. The customer receives nothing until your team clicks Approve.

Can I keep my current WhatsApp business phone number?

Yes. You can connect your current phone number to the official Meta Cloud WhatsApp Business API. If your number is currently on the WhatsApp mobile app, we provide a simple 3-step migration process so you keep your brand identity.

How are WhatsApp conversation fees handled? Who pays Meta?

Meta charges per 24-hour conversation window (utility, marketing, or service). Qefro does NOT add any markup or hidden margins on top of Meta's official charges. You link your Meta business account directly or fund your official BSP wallet at pass-through rates.

Where is our business data stored, and is it confidential?

Your customer data, ledger records, and conversations are encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256). We never train public AI models on your proprietary business records. For enterprise clients requiring Indian data localization, data residency is strictly hosted within Indian cloud regions (Mumbai/Hyderabad).

How long does integration really take? Is "5-minute setup" true?

Connecting your WhatsApp number takes about 5 minutes. Connecting an operational ERP or accounting system like Tally or Zoho typically takes 1 business day with our pre-built connectors. If you use Google Sheets, setup takes less than 30 minutes. We provide free 1-on-1 onboarding assistance with every trial.

What if my business does not have an ERP or POS system?

You don't need expensive enterprise software. Qefro has native two-way sync with Google Sheets. You can maintain your product catalog, appointments, or price lists in Google Sheets, and Qefro will instantly query, create, and update rows in real-time.

How is Qefro different from tools like Wati, Interakt, or AiSensy?

Wati, Interakt, and AiSensy are primarily WhatsApp broadcast and live-chat customer messaging tools. They send marketing blasts and let human agents reply to messages. Qefro is an operations platform: it connects directly to your software backends to execute business actions—like locking reservations in your POS, generating GST invoices, creating UPI payment requests, and reconciling payments—without human micro-management.

Can Qefro converse in Indian languages (Hindi, Tamil, Hinglish)?

Yes. Qefro understands and responds in multiple Indian languages and colloquial Hinglish. Customers can text naturally in their language of choice while your back-office data remains structured in English.

What happens after my 14-day free trial ends?

You can pick the plan that matches your monthly volume (Starter, Pro, or Growth). There is no lock-in contract, and you can cancel anytime. If you choose not to upgrade, your account transitions to read-only access with zero unexpected charges.
