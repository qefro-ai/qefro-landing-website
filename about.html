About Qefro

Quick Answer

Qefro is an AI Business Application Platform for businesses that already run software — ERP, CRM, POS, booking, commerce, or custom systems — and want to make those systems accessible through AI.

Most AI tools sit alongside business software. Qefro sits on top of it. The result: your customers get intelligent conversations across WhatsApp, website chat, voice, and your internal portal. Your team gets automation, CRM, and business actions connected to real data.

Qefro does not replace your systems. It makes them conversational.

Customer AI — knowledge-grounded conversations
Customer Hub — unified CRM across channels
Automation — events, workflows, follow-ups
SDK & Marketplace — connect any system
WhatsApp · Website · Voice · Portal
Workspace isolation & RBAC

Qefro was founded in 2024. The platform is developed by the Qefro team and is available at qefro.com. The admin console is at app.qefro.com.

Questions: support@qefro.com
